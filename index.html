About the Consultant

Meet Olayinka David Mobolaji

Founder & Lead Research Consultant

Olayinka David Mobolaji is a graduate of Federal University Oye-Ekiti and is currently pursuing a master’s degree. As founder of Damol Concept, he works closely with students and researchers to provide academic writing and research support, editing, and data analysis.

With over seven years of experience, he has supported undergraduate and postgraduate research projects, proposals, and manuscripts. His work includes preparing and editing manuscripts for local and international journals, proposal and grant-writing support, and helping clients interpret findings and meet departmental or journal requirements.

Damol Concept is committed to reliable, confidential, and professional guidance throughout the research process—helping clients improve their work, understand their findings, and present their research with confidence.
